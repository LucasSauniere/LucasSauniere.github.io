Lucas Saunière

I work on instrumental simulation and machine learning applied to slitless spectroscopy for the Euclid space telescope (ANR DISPERS).

Awards

  • 2024 — Euclid STAR Prize — Team Technical/Engineering Award. NISP filter wheel alignment and optical centering checks during the commissioning phase.

Software

  • psfcraft — PSF simulation for the NISP instrument
  • zernet — Zernike coefficient extraction from pure (noiseless) PSFs
  • spread — Zernike coefficient extraction from noisy PSFs
