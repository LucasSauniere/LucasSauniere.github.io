Aberrated PSF

Point Spread Functions

Every star seen through a telescope is blurred by its optics. The shape of that blur — the PSF — encodes the instrument's aberrations: defocus, astigmatism, coma, higher-order wavefront errors. Scroll to simulate correction.

CNN → Zernike coefficients

Learning the wavefront

A convolutional network takes a noisy PSF cutout and predicts the Zernike coefficients of the underlying wavefront. The layers light up as the signal propagates forward.

Predicted Zernike amplitudes

Zernike decomposition

The network's output is a compact, physically meaningful vector: defocus (Z₄), astigmatism (Z₅/Z₆), coma (Z₇/Z₈), spherical (Z₁₁)… Each coefficient is an RMS wavefront error in nanometres.

Applied to Euclid

The method is deployed on ESA's Euclid mission, recovering the PSF across a 0.53 deg² focal plane for weak-lensing cosmology.
